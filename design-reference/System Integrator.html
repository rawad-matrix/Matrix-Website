What is Matrix?

A Single, Accountable EPC Partner.

Matrix is a Lebanese system integrator delivering full EPC — Engineering, Procurement, and Construction — for industrial automation and energy systems. We've handled 1,200+ projects across Lebanon, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Algeria and Africa over 21 years.

Instead of stitching together a designer, a supplier, and a contractor — and inheriting the gaps between them — you sign one contract. We own the schedule, the specification, the bill of materials, and the commissioning report. If a fieldbus drops a packet at 3am, the same engineer who designed it picks up the phone.

Our Services

Engineering. Procurement. Construction.

Three disciplines, one delivery model. Each phase is owned by a Matrix lead with field experience on the equipment you're deploying.

01 Phase 1

Engineering

System architecture, control philosophy documents, P&ID review, instrument lists, panel schematics, ladder logic, and HMI/SCADA screen design — all signed off by a certified lead engineer.

  • Control system architecture
  • PLC/SCADA programming (Siemens, ABB, AB, Delta, Veichi)
  • Electrical & instrumentation design
  • P&ID and loop drawings
  • HMI and dashboard layout
  • Cybersecurity hardening (IEC 62443)
02 Phase 2

Procurement

Certified component sourcing through our authorized partner network. Bill-of-materials transparency, warranty coverage, and pre-negotiated lead times on the brands we install.

  • Authorized Siemens & ABB channels
  • Allen Bradley, Delta, Veichi inventory
  • VFDs, soft starters, contactors
  • Switchgear and protection devices
  • Field instruments and sensors
  • Spare parts contracts
03 Phase 3

Construction

Panel building, on-site installation, cabling, FAT/SAT testing, commissioning, and the operator-training handover that makes your team owners — not renters — of the new system.

  • Control panel fabrication
  • Site installation & cable routing
  • I/O loop checks
  • FAT and SAT protocols
  • Commissioning & start-up
  • Operator training & handover
How We Deliver

A Predictable Six-Stage Process.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Site survey, stakeholder interviews, existing-system audit. We document constraints before proposing solutions.

  2. 02

    Specification

    Functional design spec, control narrative, and fixed-scope proposal — typically within 5 business days.

  3. 03

    Engineering

    Detailed design, ladder logic, HMI mockups, and panel schematics. Reviewed with your team before procurement.

  4. 04

    Procurement

    BOM finalized and ordered through authorized channels. Status tracked in our client portal.

  5. 05

    Construction & FAT

    Panel build, factory acceptance testing, and pre-commissioning at the Matrix workshop.

  6. 06

    Commissioning & Handover

    On-site installation, SAT, operator training, and the start of your maintenance contract.
