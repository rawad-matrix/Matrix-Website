Delta Automation
Compact Control.

Get productive with the Delta automation ecosystem: PLCs, HMIs, and AC drives. A grounded 20-hour course aimed at engineers and technicians who specify and commission Delta hardware.

LevelBasic
Duration20 hours
FormatIn-person
Cohort8–14 trainees
LanguageEnglish / Arabic
CertificateMatrix EA

About This Course

Delta has become a go-to vendor for cost-sensitive automation projects across the region. This course gives you the practical fluency to specify, program, and service the Delta line.

Focused on the DVP, AS, and AX series PLCs with DOP-100 HMIs and MS-300 / C-series drives — the combinations Matrix deploys most often in OEM and SME projects.

Course Modules

  • 01
    Delta Product MapPLC, HMI, VFD, servo, robot families.
  • 02
    ISPSoft & WPLSoftProgramming environments and project setup.
  • 03
    Ladder Logic on DeltaBits, timers, counters, comparison.
  • 04
    CommunicationModbus, EtherCAT basics, CANopen.
  • 05
    DOP-100 HMIScreen design, alarms, recipes.
  • 06
    Delta VFDsParameterization, control via PLC.
  • 07
    Project CasesConveyor, pumping, packaging examples.
  • 08
    TroubleshootingCommon faults and recovery.

You Will Learn To

  • Choose the right Delta product for the job
  • Program a Delta PLC with confidence
  • Build a basic HMI in DOP / DIAStudio
  • Commission a Delta VFD and integrate over Modbus
PrerequisitesBasic electrical knowledge. No prior Delta experience required.
